A clickable Monday prep list: the two things to do before the L10. The developer meeting agenda moved to the [Friday Dev Call](https://blossom-ag.github.io/blossom-friday-dev-call/).

- 🗓️ **Auto-clears each new week** — always opens fresh on Monday (shows "Week of …").
- 💾 **Finish & save** archives the week into a **Past weeks** history you can reopen anytime.
- 📴 **No login, no backend** — runs entirely in your browser. Checks save on that device.

Pin the live link in a recurring weekly Google Task, and each Monday: open → check off → **Finish & save**.
